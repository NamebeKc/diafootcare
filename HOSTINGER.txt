DiaFootCare website installation

1. Extract the ZIP. Review the site and edit clinic-config.js.
   bookingUrl: your public appointment scheduler URL
   stripeUrl: your Stripe Payment Link for GBP
   paystackUrl: your Paystack Payment Page for NGN
   whatsappNumber: the consultant team's international number, digits only
   Use public HTTPS links. Keep provider secrets in their dashboards.
2. Upload the website files and assets/ to the document root of a Hostinger
   staging domain or subdomain using File Manager. Open index.html over HTTPS.
   This package uses ordinary static hosting and requires no Node.js server.
3. Check a booking in the scheduler's test environment, verify a checkout in
   each configured payment provider's test environment, and open the WhatsApp
   chat. Confirm scheduling rules, prices, concession fees and practice details
   before directing the production domain to the new site.
4. Add approved privacy and regulatory pages before launch.

The enquiry form opens an email draft using the patient's mail app. It passes
only their name and contact information. A completed appointment is confirmed
by your scheduler or your team. The checkout provider handles card details,
receipts and payment status. The site does not reconcile payments to bookings.

The hero photograph is an illustrative AI-generated image. The logo artwork
comes from the current DiaFootCare website.
