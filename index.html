Urgent diabetic foot symptoms

If you have diabetes and develop a new foot wound, signs of infection, or a hot or swollen foot, seek urgent help from your diabetic foot team, GP or NHS 111. Call 999 if severely unwell.

ABOUT DIAFOOTCARE

Preventing diabetic foot complications

DiaFootCare was founded by UK-trained podiatrist Arthur Okoye to improve access to specialist diabetic foot care. We support patients and healthcare teams across Africa, the United Kingdom and the Middle East.

Our clinical education focuses on metabolic, mechanical, pharmacological and vascular care.
